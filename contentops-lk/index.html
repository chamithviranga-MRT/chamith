Questions

Everything owners ask us before signing up.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. Plenty of our clients never appear in a single frame — we build content around the product, the process and the place. That said, if you're comfortable on camera, owner-led reels almost always perform best, and we'll coach you through it on shoot day.

What if I'm outside Colombo?

We work island-wide. For businesses outside the Western Province we either schedule a quarterly shoot day (and plan content in bulk), or set you up with a simple phone-filming guide and handle all editing remotely. Travel is quoted separately and only with your approval.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No lock-in. We work month to month and ask for 14 days' notice if you want to stop. We'd rather earn the next month than trap you in a twelve-month agreement.

How is this different from hiring a freelancer?

A freelancer gives you designs. We give you an operation — strategy, production, publishing, replies and reporting, with a team behind it. If one person is sick, your content still goes out.

Do you handle the ad budget too?

We plan and manage the campaigns; you pay Meta directly for the ad spend so you always see exactly where your money goes. Most of our clients start with Rs. 15,000–30,000 a month in spend, but you can start smaller.

Who owns the content you create?

You do. Every photo, video, design file and caption we produce is yours, delivered in a shared folder you keep access to — including after you stop working with us.

Can you write proper Sinhala and Tamil, not translated English?

Yes. Copy is written by native speakers, not run through a translator. That means the humour, the local terms and the tone actually land instead of reading like a form letter.

How quickly will I see results?

Consistency shows up in reach within 3–4 weeks and in enquiries usually within 6–8 weeks. Anyone promising you sales in the first week is guessing. We'd rather show you the real curve.
